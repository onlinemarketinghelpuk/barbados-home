In all four options

What's changed from your brief

  • Clearer, larger logo

    Up to 64px tall (was 46px), and it stays prominent as the header shrinks on scroll.

  • Flag matches the logo

    Same height as the logo, now a sharp vector with a transparent background. The old image had a white edge.

  • Full-width layout

    Content now uses up to 1760px of the screen (was 1240px), with readable line lengths kept.

  • Announcement bar

    Scrolling updates at the top of every page, with a pause button and the 24/7 emergency number.

  • Light and dark mode

    The sun/moon button in the header switches theme, and the site remembers each visitor's choice.

  • Opening hours

    Directly under the banner, with a live "Open now / Closed" badge in UK time.

  • Emergency contacts

    The 24/7 line is in the hours band, the announcement bar, the mobile contact bar and the footer.

  • Recognised & accredited

    ISO 9001, Paris MoU, QUALSHIP 21 and IMO, above all 21 Recognised Organisation logos.

What we need from you

  1. Your preferred option: A, B, C or D.
  2. The logo as a vector file (SVG, AI or EPS), to make it sharper still.
  3. Confirmation of the office opening hours, and any other emergency contacts to show.
  4. The announcements you'd like in the scrolling bar (3 to 5 to start).
  5. The full list of accreditations and affiliations, with their official logos.
