Types of inspection

Five inspections, one standard

The BMSR and our ANIs carry out several types of inspection over a ship's time on the register.

Read Bulletin B004

Bulletin B004 covers how deficiencies are dealt with, and the Special Inspection Program (SIP).

Flag State inspectionsShipping Act 1994
01 · Pre-registration
Ships over 12 years old. Assesses technical suitability for provisional registration. May be waived at the Principal Registrar's discretion.
02 · Initial
Within provisional registration (normally six months). A vessel cannot be permanently registered before it.
03 · Annual (AFSI)
Within three months either side of the anniversary date on the Certificate of Registry. Confirms registration, operational safety and pollution prevention standards are maintained.
04 · Follow-up
After a port State control inspection with more than three deficiencies, or a detention. Verifies deficiencies are rectified and measures prevent recurrence.
05 · Additional
One-off, requested by the BMSR, for example after persistent or recurring deficiencies, PSCI and AFSI findings, class and statutory surveys, ISM audits, IACS PR17 reports, reports of serious defects, or serious breaches of the MLC 2006.
