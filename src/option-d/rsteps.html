How registration works

From quotation to permanent registry

Provisional registration can be issued within hours. You then have six months to complete permanent registration.

Flag: BarbadosLondon registry
01 · Quotation
Quotations for initial (provisional) registration come from the Principal Registrar, or any of our Regional Registrars
02 · Provisional
The London registry registers the vessel provisionally, with all documents issued digitally, within hours once all documents are submitted
03 · Inspection
An initial inspection once provisionally registered, then annually, to make sure international standards are met
04 · Permanent
Six months to meet the requirements of permanent registration and receive the permanent certificate of registry
