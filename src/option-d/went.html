Shipping entities & advantages

Own and operate ships through Barbados

Investors who want to use Barbadian entities to own and operate ships can do so through either of two vehicles.

This is a summary for guidance only, not tax or legal advice. For detailed investment guidance, contact Invest Barbados, the investment promotion agency of Barbados.

Vehicle 1 · Shipping companyShipping Corporations Act · s.330
Purpose
A company that owns and operates ships, or acts as a holding entity for such a company
Income tax
Not subject to the Income Tax Act
Stamp duty
Not subject to the Stamp Duty Act
Exchange control
Not subject to the Exchange Control Act
Applies to
The company, and its non-resident directors and officers
Vehicle 2 · Approved shipping companyShipping Incentives Act
Term
15 years of concessions
Income tax
Income tax concessions on profits or gains
Dividends
Exempt from income tax when paid to residents; for non-residents, free of tax in excess of the rate paid in their country of residence
Import duty
With the Minister's consent, duty-free import of ships and articles used in the business
