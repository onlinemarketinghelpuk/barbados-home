Why register in Barbados

Registering your vessel under the Barbados flag brings an internationally respected registry, support around the clock, and tax advantages for operating through a Barbados entity.

Register a vessel Tax advantages

A respected flag

The Barbados Maritime Ship Registry is recognised as one of the world's best, with many internationally owned vessels flying the Barbados flag.

Barbados is a long-standing member of the International Maritime Organization (IMO), the United Nations agency that develops and maintains the regulatory framework for shipping: safety, environmental concerns, legal matters, technical co-operation, maritime security and the efficiency of shipping.

The BMSR is white-listed by the Paris Memorandum of Understanding on Port State Control, and is part of the US Coast Guard's QUALSHIP 21 programme, which identifies quality, well-run vessels visiting US ports.

Shipping entities & advantages

Own and operate ships through Barbados

Investors who want to use Barbadian entities to own and operate ships can do so through either of two vehicles.

Vehicle 1

Shipping company

Shipping Corporations Act · section 330

A company that owns and operates ships, or acts as a holding entity for such a company. These companies, and their non-resident directors and officers, are not subject to:

  • The Income Tax Act
  • The Stamp Duty Act
  • The Exchange Control Act

Vehicle 2

Approved shipping company

Shipping Incentives Act

15years of concessions, including:

  • Income tax concessions on profits or gains
  • Dividends paid to residents are exempt from income tax, and dividends paid to non-residents are free of tax in excess of the rate paid in their country of residence
  • With the Minister's consent, duty-free import of ships and articles used in the business

This is a summary for guidance only, not tax or legal advice. For detailed investment guidance, contact Invest Barbados, the investment promotion agency of Barbados.
