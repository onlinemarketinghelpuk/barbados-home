Registration of a ship's mortgage

Ship mortgages

A provisionally registered ship has all the privileges and entitlements of a Barbados permanently registered ship, so a loan or other valuable consideration can be recorded as a mortgage over the ship or its shares.

ABC…Z

Mortgage priority: "A" is the first-priority mortgage

More about mortgages
  • 01

    The original mortgage document is forwarded to the London Registrar or the Principal Registrar.

  • 02

    The Registrar records the mortgage, noting on each document the date and time it was recorded.

  • 03

    Each mortgage is annotated with a letter from A to Z. "A" denotes a first-priority mortgage, and the priority of later mortgages follows the letter the Registrar assigns.

  • 04

    If, within 30 days of the provisional certificate expiring, the ship has not fully complied with the requirements of registration, the mortgagee has the absolute right of sale of the ship or share, even if the mortgagor complied fully with the other requirements of the mortgage instrument.

Bareboat charter out

From Barbados to another flag

BarbadosPrimary registry→Another flagSecondary registry

A vessel registered in Barbados can be dual registered with a secondary registry under a bareboat charter out agreement, while keeping its Barbadian registration.

Bareboat charter in

From another flag to Barbados

Another flagPrimary registry→BarbadosSecondary registry

A vessel on another primary registry can be dual registered under the Barbados flag under a bareboat charter in agreement, while keeping its registration with the primary registry.
