Certified quality

The BMSR retains ISO 9001:2015 LRQA certification, having first achieved QA certification in 1996, and maintained it ever since.

This certification proves the BMSR's ability to meet shipowners' and managers' needs, and underlines our commitment to the continual improvement of our performance.

The BMSR is a premium quality flag State, offering comprehensive services for ship registration, mortgage registration and discharge transactions, ships' statutory compliance with Barbados national regulations and IMO Conventions, and seafarer endorsements.

Our in-depth experience, technical knowledge and quality system are the key to resolving any issues in a timely and appropriate manner, and to delivering on our promises.

01

Our commitments

  • Sustainability

    BMSR is committed to sustainability management and to considering climate change risks in the delivery of its services. This includes supporting measures that reduce environmental impact, promote responsible resource use, and contribute to the long-term protection of the marine environment.

  • Quality service

    BMSR is committed to providing quality services with an operating philosophy based on open communication, integrity in serving the customers, fairness and concern for the employees, and responsibility to the communities within which BMSR operates.

  • Compliance

    BMSR is committed to vessels complying with national and international regulations, to achieve continuous compliance with the maritime regulations as well as the ISO Standard under which the BMSR quality management system operates.

  • Our vision

    To exceed customer expectations for compliance, quality, safety, sustainability, cost, delivery and value, while supporting responsible environmental and climate-conscious practices within BMSR operations.

02

Customers first

We are dedicated to creating a profitable business culture based on the following principles. Customer needs are of paramount importance: BMSR's obligation is to be proactive in seeking out and defining customer needs, whilst addressing all requests expeditiously and within the legal framework of national and international requirements.

Commencing with a clear understanding and definition of customers' expectations, we work hard to be consistent in meeting or exceeding those expectations.

03

Supporting compliance

BMSR provides timely professional advice to its customers, to allow them to continue to operate their vessels in compliance with national and international requirements, by:

  • The provision of flag certificates issued by the BMSR, and statutory certificates issued by Recognised Organisations on behalf of Barbados
  • The recording of ownership by the legal adding, amending or removing of entries from the Official Register of Ships, including the entering and deletion of mortgages
  • The issue of temporary permits and permanent equivalences and exemptions, where the Conventions give such discretion to the flag Administration
  • Carrying out the above services on behalf of the Government of Barbados, which has authorised the Principal Registrar to act in these matters
  • Issuing seafarer endorsements and discharge books for seafarers employed on Barbadian vessels
  • Carrying out flag State inspections as per the Barbados Shipping Act
  • Assessing any marine accident, and carrying out marine safety investigations when required to do so
  • Flag State reporting to the IMO, as required by the Conventions
