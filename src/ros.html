Authorised to act for Barbados

Barbados has authorised Recognised Organisations to carry out statutory certification and services for ships registered in Barbados.

These services are also provided for companies operating Barbadian ships.

The current list of Recognised Organisations is shown below. Select an organisation to visit its website.

Questions about authority

Ask the registry directly

Any request for clarification about the scope or extent of a Recognised Organisation's authority to represent or act on behalf of Barbados should be made directly to the Barbados Maritime Ship Registry.
