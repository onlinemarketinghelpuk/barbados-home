  1. 0The event

    The accident happens

    Any accident causing loss of life or serious injury, or material damage to the vessel that may affect its seaworthiness.

  2. 4hDeadline

    Initial report

    The owner, operator or master sends an initial report to the BMSR by telephone, email or the online form.

  3. 24hDeadline

    Form 45

    Further details are provided on Form 45 within 24 hours of the occurrence.

The reporting duty

Who must report

Under the Barbados Shipping Act and the IMO Casualty Investigation Code.

Accident reportingBarbadian vessels
Who reports
The owner or operator, or the master of the vessel
Loss of life
Any accident resulting in loss of life
Serious injury
Any accident resulting in serious injury to any person
Material damage
Any material damage to the vessel that may affect its seaworthiness
Initial report
Within 4 hours, by telephone, email or the online form
Full details
On Form 45, within 24 hours
